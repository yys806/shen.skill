# shen.skill blog synthesis

This file is a compact synthesis of the public blog for use by shen.skill. It intentionally stores summaries and URLs instead of full article copies.

## Main themes

- The blog currently has 16 archived posts from 2025-05-07 to 2026-03-30.
- Most posts are development notes, tutorials, tool inventories, server/deployment notes, AI tooling guides, and personal project retrospectives.
- The writing pattern is practical: explain why a tool/project was made, list modules or steps, include links/screenshots, then end with a short reflection.
- The projects show a strong "see something interesting -> replicate/build it -> deploy it -> write a note" loop.
- Personal development is tied to usefulness and self-improvement: paper reading tools for research, navigation/tool sites for efficiency, personal intro pages for external presentation, and storage/accounting tools for life management.

## Personality signals from blog

- High tool-making drive: frequently turns personal friction into a small website or utility.
- Strong replication-to-learning style: openly starts from imitation/reproduction, then customizes and integrates into personal workflow.
- Practical deployment orientation: cares about domains, Netlify, GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, Supabase, server reuse, and maintainability.
- Self-presentation awareness: maintains blog, intro site, portfolio links, certificates, projects and awards as an outward-facing profile.
- Research anxiety plus persistence: in the paper-reading-tool post, the project comes from a period of wanting to quit research but deciding to persist for the experience.
- Relationship memory and symbolic making: couple blog and custom JLCPCB card show a tendency to materialize emotional bonds into artifacts.
- Efficiency obsession: navigation, personal homepage, storage/accounting, command notes, tool collections, and AI guides all serve lower-friction daily work.
